# Code of Conduct

**Project Maintainer:** Robert Edward Evans  
**Contact:** evansrobert517@gmail.com

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone in the Homebrew Core community will be treated with respect and dignity.

## Expected Behavior

We expect all community members to:
- Be respectful and inclusive
- Listen actively to others
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on solutions, not blame
- Contribute positively to discussions
- Respect differing opinions and experiences

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Offensive comments or personal attacks
- Trolling or deliberate disruption
- Publishing others' private information
- Any form of abuse or unwelcome conduct

## Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:
- Warning or temporary ban from the community
- Removal of comments or posts
- Permanent removal from the project

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to **evansrobert517@gmail.com** with:
- Description of the incident
- Involved parties
- Any relevant links or evidence
- Your contact information

## Acknowledgment

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces managed by the Homebrew Core project, including GitHub, discussions, and any other community channels.

Thank you for helping maintain a positive and welcoming community!
